## Finite process gates

`tools/translated_scope_gate.py` implements two ready/go exchanges for `native/tests/translated_owner_scope.cpp`. The parent supplies a fresh 32-digit hexadecimal nonce and a private directory. Ready packets are at most 16 KiB; go/refuse packets are at most 256 bytes. Both have exact schemas, a sequence of one or two and no commands. Files are regular, bounded, create-only and checked without following symlinks/reparse points. Each wait is limited to 30 seconds. Refusal or timeout causes normal owned-fixture cleanup; the parent records the actual child return and never invents success or sends a termination signal.

Gate one occurs after the original setup/upload fence is consumed and before either new case. Gate two is intended after both case readbacks/fences, while the device is still alive. The model requires the same admitted host PID/start cookie at both gates, retains previous claims and rejects later uninventoried scopes. These gates do not imply zero prior setup submissions.

The collector reads only PIDs in at most eight nonce-matching scope claims. Per PID, it brackets exact NUL-separated cmdline and maps snapshots with bounded stat field-22 start cookies. Limits are 4 KiB for stat, 64 KiB for cmdline and 1 MiB/8,192 lines for maps. Expected exact paths, device/inode and parent backing-file pins cover five roles: the owned executable, d3d12, d3d12core, DXGI and the observer. Namespace paths are declared in advance. No basename heuristic, global `/proc` scan, environment dump or process-memory read is used.

Missing/changed facts, denied reads, PID reuse, ambiguous candidates or a partial potential candidate keep correlation unknown. Windows PID is diagnostic and is never compared numerically with host PID. Cmdline is mutable and maps identify backing files; even a successful process correlation would not attest loaded code bytes or identify a Unix VkDevice.

## Intended explicit query witness

The new PE reuses the frozen owned shaders and fixture helpers for two planned cases: direct A/clamp, then B copied into the selected descriptor after binding was recorded. Its reviewed typed `ID3D12DXVKInteropDevice` prefix records actual QueryInterface/GetVulkanHandles HRESULTs, checks bounded extension/feature results and prepares two distinct scratch spans for original sampler `vkGetDescriptorEXT` calls. The first witness is intended after gate one; the second after the cases and before gate two. The original query is void; no HRESULT or VkResult is fabricated for it.

`tools/translated_owner_witness.py` joins exact original typed operands, phase, output address/size, returned bytes and closed scope/device lifetimes. The reviewed x64 Wine source converts the dispatchable device at its thunk boundary while preserving these query operands. This is a source contract, not proof that installed binaries match that source. Equality, proximity or offsets between PE and Unix dispatchable handles establish no mapping. A valid pair of process reports alone cannot establish device ownership. Original-argument auditing and the scoped lifecycle audit must also pass.

The witness model is tested on supplied owned snapshots. No successful translated query witness, translated Unix-device owner, selected descriptor placement, current contents, GPU read or API-global absence is established by this milestone.
